FIGURE VOCABULARY
IDLE
Between lines. Small vertical bob.
TYPING
Locked in, leaning into the line.
WINDUP
Fist cocked. Fires near line completion.
STRIKE
On completion. Damage burst fires here.
HIT-TAKEN
Head snaps back. Never shakes the text.
STAGGER
Uncorrected error. Self-damage, off balance.
SPECIAL
Four clean lines. 1.8x on the next one.
VICTORY
Round or match won.
DEFEAT
KO or timer loss.
