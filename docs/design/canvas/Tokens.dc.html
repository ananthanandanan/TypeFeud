NOTHING SIGNALS BY HUE ALONE
Blue and orange are the Okabe–Ito pair, distinguishable under every form of colourblindness. On top of that, you are always on the left and errors always carry an underline — so position and shape carry the meaning even with colour removed entirely.
EFFECTS NEVER TOUCH THE TEXT
Screen shake, sabotage jitter and impact bursts render behind the typing surface or outside its bounding box. The panel is opaque and sits above the stage for exactly this reason. When in doubt, reduce the effect.
