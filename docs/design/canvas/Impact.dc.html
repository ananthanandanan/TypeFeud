BOTH SCREENS AGREE FOR FREE
Contact is driven by one discrete line.resolved message, not by per-frame positions. There is no collision to reconcile and nothing extra on the wire — the animation is still derived from progress, exactly as §4.3 requires.
SIMULTANEOUS HITS CLASH
Both players can finish a line in the same instant. Rather than letting the figures tangle, both lunge and meet in the middle — one shared burst, both recoil. Cheaper than queueing, and it looks deliberate instead of broken.
THE STAGE NEVER REACHES THE TEXT
Lunges, bursts and shake are confined to the stage band above the typing panel, which is opaque and painted over it. A haymaker can throw a figure across the screen and still not move a single character the player is reading.
